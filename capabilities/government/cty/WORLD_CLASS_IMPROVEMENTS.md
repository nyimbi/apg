# County / Devolved Services — World-Class Improvements

### I1. Permit Renewal Workflow | Lifecycle Management | Issued permits expire annually; currently there is no renewal path, forcing officers to re-issue from scratch and breaking audit continuity | Add `renew_permit()` that clones the issued permit, resets issue/expiry dates, links to predecessor via `renewed_from`, and validates fee payment | GovOS (Philippines e-Gov), eCitizen Kenya

### I2. Welfare Payment Disbursement Ledger | Financial Accuracy | Approved welfare amounts are stored but payments are never recorded, making it impossible to audit actual cash-out vs approved amounts | Add `disburse_welfare_payment()` with Decimal amounts, disbursement channel (mobile_money/bank/cash), reference number, and cumulative `total_disbursed_kes` tracking per application | GovTech Singapore (CareShield), South Africa SASSA digital

### I3. Revenue Reconciliation & Variance Report | Fiscal Accountability | Collected revenue can drift from budget targets with no early-warning mechanism; IDP requires quarterly variance reports | Add `reconcile_revenue()` that compares collected confirmed revenue against budget allocations for a period and returns variance, coverage ratio, and per-type shortfall list | Kenya IFMIS, Oracle Financials for Government

### I4. Permit Inspection Scheduling | Compliance Enforcement | Building/health permits require on-site inspection before issuance but the current flow jumps directly from submission to issuance, bypassing mandatory site visits | Add `schedule_permit_inspection()` and `record_inspection_outcome()` with inspector assignment, scheduled date, checklist results, pass/fail decision, and photo evidence URLs | Singapore CORENET, UK Planning Portal

### I5. Health Facility Bed Census | Resource Allocation | County health planners cannot allocate patients or resources without knowing current bed occupancy; `beds` field is static and never updated | Add `update_facility_bed_census()` to record available, occupied, and reserved beds with timestamps, and `get_bed_census_report()` for cross-facility capacity view | Kenya KHIS2, WHO Health Facility Registry

### I6. Citizen Feedback & Satisfaction Scoring | Service Quality | Ticket resolution quality is opaque; no feedback loop means officers cannot distinguish genuinely resolved vs dismissed tickets from citizen perspective | Add `submit_citizen_feedback()` linked to closed tickets with rating (1-5), comment, and `calculate_service_satisfaction_score()` aggregating per sub-county | Nairobi City County Huduma Portal, Mombasa County App

### I7. Market Stall Fee Collection Cycle | Revenue Completeness | Market stalls have a `monthly_fee_kes` but no mechanism to collect or track monthly payments, causing arrears to accumulate silently | Add `collect_stall_fee()` to record monthly fee payments against a stall, `get_stall_arrears()` to compute unpaid months, and `list_stalls_in_arrears()` for enforcement | Kisumu County Market Management, Cape Town Market System

### I8. County Budget Expenditure Tracking | Fiscal Control | `expenditures` dict is initialized but never written to; budget `spent_kes` is static and the capability cannot demonstrate financial discipline to the CAG | Add `record_expenditure()` with Decimal amounts, LPO/invoice reference, department, and `get_budget_utilization()` returning burn rate and projected end-of-year status | Kenya e-procurement (IFMIS), IPSAS-compliant systems

### I9. Contractor Performance Scoring | Procurement Integrity | Contractors are registered and assigned to tickets but performance is never evaluated, enabling blacklisted or poor contractors to keep receiving work | Add `rate_contractor_performance()` per completed ticket with score (1-5) and narrative, `get_contractor_scorecard()` aggregating average score, jobs completed, and complaints | National Construction Authority (Kenya), World Bank STEP procurement

### I10. Ward-Level Service Delivery Analytics | Equity Monitoring | Dashboard aggregates at county level, hiding intra-county inequities that the Ward Development Fund requires to be tracked separately | Add `ward_equity_report()` returning per-ward breakdown of revenue collected, permits issued, welfare approvals, open tickets, and a composite equity index | Kenya Ward Development Fund, USAID LocalGov analytics

### I11. Automated Permit Expiry Notifications Queue | Proactive Compliance | Businesses do not receive advance warning before permits expire, resulting in avoidable compliance violations and lost revenue for the county | Add `get_expiring_permits()` returning permits expiring within N days with contact details, and `mark_expiry_notification_sent()` to track outreach | eCitizen Kenya, ZimRA business portal

### I12. Bulk Revenue Import from M-Pesa Paybill | Interoperability | Paybill settlements arrive as batch files from Safaricom; currently each must be captured manually one-by-one, creating backlog and data-entry errors | Add `bulk_import_revenues()` accepting a list of raw paybill records, applying deduplication by receipt number, and returning import summary with success/error counts | Safaricom Daraja API, Kenya Revenue Authority iTax bulk upload

### I13. Public Expenditure Transparency Feed | Open Government | County OpenData requirements mandate machine-readable expenditure feeds for civil society monitoring; current data is locked inside service state | Add `get_transparency_feed()` returning a sanitised, structured list of approved expenditures and budget utilisation, excluding PII, suitable for publishing to the county open data portal | Kenya Open Data Portal, AfricanOpenData initiative

### I14. Health Referral Chain Management | Care Continuity | Patients at dispensaries requiring higher-level care have no formal referral tracking; transfers are verbal, creating gaps in the county health information system | Add `create_health_referral()` linking source facility, destination facility, patient, clinical reason, urgency, and `update_referral_status()` for tracking acceptance/arrival | Kenya HIS (KEMR), WHO FHIR referral resource

### I15. Devolved Environmental Inspection Registry | Regulatory Completeness | Building and signage permits have environmental conditions but no inspection registry exists, leaving the county exposed to NEMA audit failures | Add `register_environmental_inspection()` for EIA compliance checks on permitted sites with inspector ID, findings, compliance status, and `list_environmental_violations()` for enforcement action | NEMA Kenya, South Africa DFFE compliance portal
