# Budgeting and Forecasting User Guide

## Planning Workflow

1. Create the budget with owner, fiscal year, currency, and valid planning period.
2. Add budget lines for revenue, expense, capital, or headcount plans.
3. Submit the budget after all required lines are present.
4. Approve the submitted budget with an independent approver.
5. Create forecasts from trend, driver, statistical, or manual methods.
6. Record forecast points for each planning period.
7. Build scenarios with probability and driver assumptions.
8. Record actuals and review material variances.
9. Start collaboration sessions when planners need shared review.

## Screens

- Dashboard: tenant summary, lifecycle counts, and stream metadata.
- Budgets: budget creation, submission, and approval status.
- Budget Lines: planning-line entry by account, type, amount, period, and cost center.
- Forecasts: forecast setup and forecast-point review.
- Scenarios: probability and driver assumption management.
- Variances: budget-to-actual review and material variance handling.
- Approvals: submitted budget queue.
- Collaboration: planning session participation.
- Agents: BFC-agent registration and privileged-action review.

## BFC Agents

BFC agents can assist with planning review and preparation work. Supported runtimes are `codex`, `claude_code`, `opencode`, and `pi`. Privileged BFC actions still require human approval before execution.
