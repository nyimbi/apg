# EOD Processing Engine — World-Class Improvements

### I1. Penalty Interest on Overdue Loans | Credit Risk | Missed instalments should accrue penalty at a contractual rate (typically 2–5× the normal rate) from the day after the due date. Without this, P&L understates delinquency cost and regulatory NPL calculations are wrong. | Compute `days_overdue × overdue_balance × penalty_rate / 365` per loan; post to Penalty Income GL; update the loan's `penalty_accrued` balance in the same accrual batch window. | Temenos T24, Finastra Equation, Mambu

### I2. IFRS 9 ECL Staging and Provision Posting | Regulatory / IFRS | IFRS 9 requires classifying loans into Stage 1 (12-month ECL), Stage 2 (lifetime ECL — SICR), Stage 3 (credit-impaired) each reporting period. Provision deltas must be posted to the GL daily. Without daily staging, period-end provisions create P&L cliff effects and CBK inspection findings. | For each loan: evaluate SICR triggers (DPD > 30, rating downgrade, covenant breach). Move stage accordingly. Calculate ECL = PD × LGD × EAD × discount factor. Post delta (increase/decrease) to Impairment Expense / Loan Loss Reserve. | Temenos Transact ECL Module, Finastra Credit Risk

### I3. Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) Computation | Regulatory Liquidity | Basel III mandates daily LCR monitoring: HQLA / Net cash outflows ≥ 100%. Missing daily LCR means compliance gaps that attract CBK/central bank penalties and restrict dividend capacity. | Classify assets into HQLA Level 1/2A/2B haircut buckets. Compute 30-day stressed net outflows by product category. Persist LCR to regulatory reporting store. Alert if LCR < 105% (early warning) or < 100% (breach). | Temenos, FIS Profile, Oracle FLEXCUBE

### I4. Intraday Nostro Reconciliation | Operational Risk | Unreconciled nostro entries are a primary source of fraud and operational loss (average £1.4M per incident, KPMG 2024). Daily automated reconciliation catches breaks within 24 hours vs the industry-average 3.5 days. | Fetch statement lines from SWIFT MT940/MT950 or ISO 20022 camt.053. Match to internal GL nostro entries by amount, value date, and reference. Flag unmatched items as exceptions; escalate items > configured threshold to operations. | Finastra Fusion Cash Management, Intellect SEEC

### I5. Tiered Interest Rate Bands | Product Management | Most deposit products tier rates: e.g., 0–50K at 3%, 50K–500K at 4.5%, >500K at 5.5%. Flat-rate accrual mis-prices deposits, erodes NIM, and breaches product T&Cs. | Break each account balance into rate bands defined in a `rate_schedule` table. Sum accruals across bands using correct tier amounts. Store band breakdown in accrual audit log for transparency. | Temenos T24, nCino, Finacle

### I6. Cheque Clearance and Float Management | Operations | Deposited cheques sit in float (unavailable funds) for a clearing cycle (T+1/T+2). Without automated float movement, banks either over-restrict customer funds or extend unearned credit. | At BOD, promote float items that have reached their clearance date to available balance. Post entries to Float GL and customer account. Mark cheque as `cleared`. Reject and reverse for returned cheques. | FIS Horizon, Jack Henry Banno, Finastra

### I7. Automated Regulatory Return Generation | Compliance | CBK, RBA, and similar regulators require standard statistical returns (BSL02, BSL03, NBK600) on specific frequencies. Manual preparation is error-prone and late submissions attract fines. | On trigger dates (month-end, quarter-end), render required regulatory forms from aggregated GL data. Validate totals against internal control figures. Stamp with digital signature. Push to regulatory submission endpoint or export to XBRL. | Oracle Financial Services Analytical Applications, Wolters Kluwer OneSumX

### I8. Non-Working Day Roll Logic | Calendar Management | Financial contracts use day-count conventions (Modified Following, Preceding, Actual/Actual). Accrual batches running on a weekend must correctly identify the prior/next business day for due-date calculations. | Load business calendar for each currency/jurisdiction at service startup. For each date-sensitive operation (fee due date, loan instalment, maturity), apply the contract's day-count convention before computing amounts. | Temenos, Murex, Bloomberg BPIPE

### I9. Parallel Job Execution with Dependency Graph | Performance | 10 sequential jobs on a large portfolio (10M+ accounts) can exceed a 4-hour processing window. Banks that fail to close EOD before market open face operational crises. | Model jobs as a DAG. Jobs with no cross-job data dependency (e.g., fee posting and dormancy check) can run concurrently with `asyncio.gather`. Jobs that depend on earlier results (reports) wait on their predecessors. Configurable concurrency limit. | Finastra Payments Hub, TCS BaNCS, Temenos Infinity

### I10. Audit Trail with Immutable Journal Entries | Audit / Compliance | All financial postings must be immutable and traceable to the EOD run that generated them. Without this, regulatory auditors cannot reconstruct the balance sheet at any historical date. | Each batch-generated posting carries metadata: `source_job`, `run_id`, `processing_date`, `idempotency_key`, `created_by=system`. Store in an append-only journal table (Postgres with row-level `created_at`, no `UPDATE`/`DELETE`). | Core banking: SAP Banking, Oracle FLEXCUBE

### I11. Configurable Dormancy Rules per Product Type | Product / Regulatory | Dormancy thresholds differ by product and jurisdiction (CBK: savings 12 months, current 24 months, investment 36 months). A single global threshold miscategorises accounts and creates AML/FATF risk. | Define `dormancy_policy` table keyed on `(tenant_id, product_category)` with `threshold_days`, `pre_notice_days`, `escheatment_days`. Apply per-account based on its product type during the dormancy batch. | Temenos, Oracle FLEXCUBE, Mambu

### I12. NPA (Non-Performing Asset) Classification | Credit Risk / Regulatory | RBI/CBK require loan facilities overdue > 90 days to be classified as NPA. NPA accounts attract 100% provisioning requirement and must cease accruing interest into income. | During loan repayment batch: evaluate DPD for all facilities. Promote to NPA if DPD ≥ 90. Suspend interest accrual (reverse to sundry, not P&L). Post 100% provision. Generate NPA report for regulatory submission. | Finacle, Oracle FLEXCUBE, TCS BaNCS

### I13. Multi-Currency Balance Sheet Generation | Reporting / FX | A bank reporting in USD with EUR/GBP positions needs a consolidated balance sheet translating all CCY balances at the correct spot/average rate. Without it, the P&L and balance sheet do not reconcile. | At month-end, for each FCY account translate ending balance to reporting currency using the closing spot rate. Translate income/expense lines at average rate. Show FX translation reserve in OCI. | SAP Bank Analyser, Oracle FCCM, FIS Risk and Compliance

### I14. Sweep Optimisation and Zero-Balance Accounting | Treasury | Corporate clients with multiple sub-accounts benefit from automated ZBA sweeps that concentrate surplus cash into a master account overnight. This optimises interest earned and reduces daylight overdraft fees. | After all EOD postings are complete, evaluate ZBA sweep rules: if sub-account balance > threshold, transfer surplus to master. If sub-account is negative and master has surplus, fund it. Post offsetting entries. Update next sweep parameters. | Finastra Cash Management, TreasuryXpress, FIS Integrity

### I15. SLA Monitoring and Alerting | Operational Excellence | EOD must complete within a contracted window (typically 23:00–05:00 local time). Processing delays cascade to ATM downtime, failed settlements, and missed regulatory cutoffs. Proactive alerting is absent in most mid-tier cores. | Record `started_at` per run. During processing, publish progress events. If elapsed time > configured SLA threshold (e.g., 70% of window consumed with < 50% jobs complete), emit a `eod.sla_at_risk` event and page on-call. On completion, compare actual duration to SLA; persist SLA compliance record. | Temenos Operations Manager, FIS Integrated Banking, Oracle BPM
