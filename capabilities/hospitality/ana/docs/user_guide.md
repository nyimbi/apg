# ANA User Guide

## Overview

The Hospitality Analytics capability (hos_ana) provides the performance intelligence layer for hotel operations, covering the key industry metrics: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, GOP PAR, segment mix, booking pace, and guest satisfaction (NPS).

## Key Metrics

- **Occupancy Rate**: Occupied rooms / total rooms × 100
- **ADR (Average Daily Rate)**: Room revenue / occupied rooms
- **RevPAR (Revenue Per Available Room)**: Room revenue / total rooms = ADR × Occupancy Rate
- **GOP PAR (Gross Operating Profit Per Available Room)**: GOP / total rooms
- **NPS (Net Promoter Score)**: (Promoters - Detractors) / total surveys × 100

## Segments

Track booking mix across: leisure, corporate, group, government, airline, and other segments. Understand which segments drive rate vs volume.

## Pace Reporting

Pace reports capture how many rooms are on the books for a future date, measured at different points in time. Compare current pickup pace against the same time last year to identify over-/under-pace situations early.

## Benchmarking

Record RevPAR, ADR, and occupancy indices against market averages and defined competitive sets. Index > 100 = above market; index < 100 = below market.

## Guest Satisfaction

Post-stay surveys capture scores across rooms, service, food, and cleanliness. NPS categories: Promoters (9-10), Passives (7-8), Detractors (1-6).
